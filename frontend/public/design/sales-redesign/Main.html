One place for a sales user's whole day

Today a sales user's work is spread over ~30 sidebar entries in Sales, Calls, Reports, HRMS and Messenger, and the same number (calls, callbacks, prospects) is counted differently on different screens. The new panel has 8 entries, and every number has one meaning and one source.

One number, one meaning — used on every screen

DialledA call placed to the customer, by the auto dialer, a callback or by hand. Counted once per call.
TalkedThe customer answered and the user joined the call, talk time above 0 s. Only this counts as "spoke".
Not reachedRejected, busy, no answer, switched off or a carrier error, shown with the exact reason.
CallbackA call promised to the customer for a date and time. Overdue · Due today · Upcoming · Done (talked) · Missed (3 tries, not reached).
ProspectTalked and interested: Connected (qualified), Interested, Demo Booking or Prospects stage. One lead = one prospect, never counted twice.
Follow-upFuture Prospect with a next date. Shows on My Day the day it is due.
EnrolledPayment done. The only "win".
Dialer timeMinutes with the dialer on and the user Available or on a call. Breaks and Paused excluded. Same figure as attendance.
Not doneAnything the user owed today and did not do: overdue callbacks, follow-ups past due, calls with no response saved, chats unanswered for over 30 min.
